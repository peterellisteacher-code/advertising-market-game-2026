## Decision

Proceed with independently authored, deliberately simplified SVG geometry. Preserve large blank artwork surfaces and treat rims, lids, straps, pockets, handles, and closures as optional components. Revisit stock downloads only if a later body cannot be made convincing from trusted-code primitives; any such download requires a separate manifest entry and licence record.
