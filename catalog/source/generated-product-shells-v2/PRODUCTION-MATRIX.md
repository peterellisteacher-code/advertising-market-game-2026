# Raster template production matrix

Target: at least 2,000 accepted, individually sliced assets. Plan for 2,500 raw motifs so a 20% visual/technical rejection rate does not shrink the classroom catalogue below target.

All counts below assume one strict 5 x 5 PNG source sheet with 25 isolated cells. A contact sheet is raw source inventory, not a finished catalogue asset.

## Locked runtime art contract

- Raster only: lossless square PNG source sheet; transparent RGBA PNG individual outputs. No SVG runtime art.
- Exact flat `#00ff00` source background; no near-green subject pixels.
- Dark, clean, uniform outline; white fill; minimal structural grey.
- One declared sheet type: `base`, `add-on`, `scene`, or `placement-frame`.
- Base sheets: blank personalisable face around 30% or more of the visible body.
- Add-on sheets: isolated parts at the same view, line weight, and scale family as their bases.
- No brand, text, logo, watermark, finished advertisement, person, scenery, foliage, cast shadow, ground ellipse, or baked background on base/add-on sheets.
- Exactly one item per regular cell; at least 10% safe padding; no tiny/huge subject mixing.

## Acceptance pipeline

Each cell must pass: grid-boundary containment, chroma spill suppression, minimum usable dimensions, transparent-edge/halo checks on light/dark/warm/saturated backgrounds, text/brand review, malformed-object review, duplicate review, type/category metadata, search tags, and base/add-on compatibility metadata. Rejected cells remain preserved in source but do not enter the classroom catalogue.
