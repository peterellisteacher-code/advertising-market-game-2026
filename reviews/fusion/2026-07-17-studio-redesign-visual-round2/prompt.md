# Independent visual review: Advertising Market Studio redesign

Review the two supplied production screenshots as immutable evidence. This is a fresh, independent review; do not assume prior review findings or a preferred verdict.

## Product context

- Browser-hosted Godot classroom game for paired Year 10 students.
- This screen is the in-game creative studio for inventing a product and designing its advertising.
- The experience must feel like a playful game/editor, not a worksheet, assignment portal, or administrative dashboard.
- Screenshot state: the reusable tumbler body and lid have been selected in the Build drawer, but the product has not yet been placed on the blank advertising canvas.
- Target classroom displays include 1366×768 laptops and 1920×1080 desktops.
- Students work in pairs sharing one computer.
- Raster product artwork is intentionally an editable outline/template rather than a finished advertisement.

## Acceptance constraints to assess

1. No text may be clipped, orphaned, collide with controls, overflow its box, or become unreadably small at either supplied viewport.
2. The campaign canvas must be the visual protagonist, with editing controls discoverable but subordinate.
3. Blank space must read as purposeful creative workspace or clear breathing room, not as missing content or a broken layout.
4. A student should immediately understand the next useful action and the relationship between product construction, canvas creation, audience, price, route, and AIDA progression.
5. Navigation must avoid nested-scroll or endless-library framing and should resemble proven creative tools/configurators in interaction hierarchy.
6. Evaluate visual hierarchy, density, sizing, spacing, typography, contrast, affordance, consistency, delight, game identity, and classroom legibility.
7. Compare the composition and interaction model to relevant conventions from current products such as Figma, Adobe Express, Canva-style editors, Hero Forge, Nike By You, IKEA Kreativ, The Sims Build Mode, or similar editors/configurators. Do not demand superficial imitation; identify which conventions are or are not successfully translated.
8. Distinguish the intentionally empty pre-placement canvas from genuinely wasteful or confusing dead space.

## Required verdict

Return one concise Opus synthesis containing:

- Overall PASS or FAIL for this visual milestone.
- Findings ordered by severity, with screenshot-specific evidence.
- What is already working and should be preserved.
- The smallest high-leverage changes needed before the next milestone.
- Explicit comparison to at least three relevant editor/configurator conventions.
- A final acceptance checklist for 1366×768 and 1920×1080.

Do not provide implementation code. Do not infer behavior that is not visible in the screenshots.
