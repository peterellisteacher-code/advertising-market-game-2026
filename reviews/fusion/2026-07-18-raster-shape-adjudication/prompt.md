# Visual adjudication of product-template assets

Review the 15 supplied raster assets as a fresh, independent visual assessment. They belong to a Year 10 classroom advertising game in which pairs choose, recolour, combine, label, and advertise products. The images must therefore be immediately recognisable, structurally coherent, visually clean, and usable as neutral illustrated templates rather than finished advertisements.

Assess each image independently at both its supplied resolution and the likely small-card thumbnail scale. Do not infer a preferred verdict from the fact that it was supplied. For each numbered item, return exactly one disposition:

- **KEEP** — the shape and current title are clear enough for classroom use;
- **RETITLE** — the drawing is coherent and useful, but the current title materially misdescribes what is visible; include a precise replacement title;
- **REGENERATE** — geometry, part relationships, silhouette, count, or small-scale readability is materially unreliable; state the minimum visual requirement for a replacement.

Judge recognisability, silhouette, internal geometry, plausible part anatomy and count, title-image agreement, isolation from unintended neighbouring objects, usefulness as a recolourable/composable shell or add-on, and thumbnail readability. Ignore minor stylistic variation that does not impede use. Do not redesign the catalogue, recommend a different art style, or assess unrelated game UI.
