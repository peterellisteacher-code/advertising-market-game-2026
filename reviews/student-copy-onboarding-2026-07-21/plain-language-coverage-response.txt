

(a) CRITIQUE

Request type: coverage question (start, roles, progress, "done").

**Start — can a pair begin immediately?**
- The lobby orients: it asks for the pair name, then Practice or a classroom code, and the "Choose a team name to begin" / "Start the game with your pair" copy states the first action.
- The lobby does not orient: the game is pair-based, but no surface states whether both partners sit at one device, take turns on one device, or join from two devices. "With your pair" is unexplained, so a single student cannot tell what setup is required before clicking Start.
- The account gate precedes the lobby but no surface explains the relation: the account stores a private save, the pair name identifies the team on the floor; the two are distinct, and the candidate never states this.

**Roles — are Art Director and Strategist introduced?**
- Defined once in the studio copy ("Art Director: one visible image change"; "Strategist: one visible message change") and in the persistent top strip ("Pass control to the Art Director / Strategist").
- Not introduced before the studio opens: the lobby, the Level 1 run screen, and the Level 1 clue ("name the product, build it, and identify its target customer") never name the two roles. A pair opens the studio, then learns there are two roles, then learns they must swap.
- "Swap roles at least once" is a checklist item but the surface never explains what swapping does — it does not state that swapping changes which partner's action the top strip names.

**Progress — is it visible?**
- Visible: the top strip names the current level, the active role, that role's exact immediate action, round progress ("no visible change / 1 visible change / N visible changes"), and the swap control. Save state ("Saved · revision", "Save paused") is named. Lock → Next level → Build market card is a stated sequence.
- Not separated cleanly: round progress (changes made this round) and level completion (all level requirements met) sit on the same strip. No surface tells a pair that round progress is not the level's stop-condition. A pair that has made one visible change but not named the product, chosen an audience brief, or swapped roles has no signal that the level is not yet done.
- Per-level requirements are listed as a checklist ("Choose an audience brief / Add a product name / Add a price / Link one choice to attention / interest / desire / action / Swap roles at least once / The Art Director needs one visible image change / The Strategist needs one visible message change"), but no surface tells a pair the checklist is the level's stop-condition or how the items tick off.

**"Done" — is the completion object clear per level and across the game?**
- Done per level: "Lock this level" appears when the level is ready; "Level locked. Ready for the next reveal" confirms the lock; "Next level" appears as the only further control. This is clear.
- Done across Levels 1–3: "Build the market card" appears after the final level is locked. The card-build step is named but not scaffolded: no surface tells a pair that the card compiles the Level 1 product, the Level 2 AIDA moves, and the Level 3 price/route/features/finish into one display, or that Lock is not the same as Build.
- Done in the market: "Finish shopping" appears when "spend at least $80 across products from at least two different teams" is met. The wallet surfaces show "%s remaining" and "%d of 2 different sellers backed" live, but the $80 rule is not on the same surface as the wallet — it lives in the body copy that the wallet tooltip may or may not surface. A shopper can see they have not yet met the rule but cannot read the rule on the surface where the spend is happening.

**Small, progressively revealed actions — are clues broken into single steps?**
- Progressive: the studio opens on Build with the role-specific immediate action on the top strip; each tool panel carries its own next-action copy; the AIDA playbook reveals each move one at a time; the market route reveals zone, media, and values step by step; status surfaces name the next missing requirement.
- Not progressive: the Level 1 clue packs three actions ("name the product, build it, and identify its target customer"). The Level 2 clue packs four AIDA moves plus a "before the buzzer" reference — and "the buzzer" is not carried elsewhere in the candidate. The Level 3 clue packs four decisions (price, route, features, visual finish). The level-level clue is the first sentence a pair reads, and it asks for three, four, or four things at once.
- Not progressive: the round 0 clue ("swap once; each player then makes one visible change") introduces the swap requirement after the level has already opened, not before. A pair that does not read this line will not know swapping is part of the loop.

**Two notable scaffold gaps beyond the four coverage questions**
- The Image Lab's asleep/awake state is named ("Ask your teacher to wake the Image Lab"; "Image Lab is asleep. Built-in tools still work"), but no surface states which level or step the Image Lab is needed for, so a pair cannot tell whether to ask the teacher to wake it before starting Level 1 or only when an Image tool is reached.
- The account gate's "Teacher only / Create a pair login" path is named, but the relation between a created pair login and a live room seat is not stated on the surface. A teacher who has just created a pair login cannot tell, from any single screen, what the pair must do next to join the room.
